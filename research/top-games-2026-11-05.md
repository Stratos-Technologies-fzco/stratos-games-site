# Top games today: what is winning (US iPhone, 5 Nov 2026)

Source: App Store top charts, US iPhone, Games, 5 Nov 2026 (250 free, 250 paid, 200 top grossing).
The full sheet with numbers was emailed to the family. It is not kept here because this repo is public.

## The one line

Puzzle games are where new games break in. Pick one, and make it very good.

## Games worth playing to learn from

- **Meowdoku!** (#1 free): sudoku with cats. Simple idea, lovely look.
- **Block Out!** (#2 free) and **Magic Sort!** (#3 free): color sort, top 3 for months.
- **Block Blast!** (#6 free): same type as our Bloxplode.
- **Parking Loop** (#9 free, Sep 2026): new and climbing fast.
- **Rotate Rings** (#8 free, Aug 2026): one-touch puzzle, new.
- **Arrows: Puzzle Escape** (#68 free): same type as our Arrow Puzzle.
- **Chess.com** (#5 free) and **Sudoku.com**: learning games that people play every day.

## What the top games share

- One idea you understand in 3 seconds, with no text to read.
- A level or a daily puzzle that is done in 1 to 3 minutes.
- A clear win moment: sound, burst, stars.
- A reason to come back tomorrow: streak, daily puzzle, next level.
- Look and feel polished everywhere: no rough screens.
