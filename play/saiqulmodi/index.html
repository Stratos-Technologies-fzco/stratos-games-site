Jet vs Missile

Fly a stealth jet through the sky while launchers on the ground fire ever-smarter homing missiles at you.

Launchers on the ground fire homing missiles that climb, turn and chase your jet. A missile never shoots: it hits you or blows itself up right next to you, so shoot it down, raise your shield, drop flares or vanish with stealth, and never fly into the ground. Every 5 levels a new nation's arsenal joins the fight: its own launchers, with multi-barrel salvos and coloured trails, and its own missiles and bombs for your jet. From level 10 hidden underground silos fire without warning until your FLIR pod finds them, and you get heat-seeking missiles. Attack drones fly out of drone launchers, and your jet's auto-gun shoots them down by itself. Fly yourself in MANUAL or let the autopilot and auto-aim help in AUTO. Against the computer you get +15% in everything, and you choose an EASY or NORMAL AI. Pause any time, and continue from your best level. Levels 1 to 100 plus endless overdrive, and a duel where the missile has to ram the jet, against a friend or the AI. Play with touch on a phone, keyboard and mouse, or a game controller. The battlefield is as wide as your screen. Phone mode, full page: https://stratos-technologies-fzco.github.io/stratos-games-site/games/saiqulmodi/index.html?mobile

How to play

Phone (sideways): drag your left thumb to fly, hold FIRE (aims itself), hold SHIELD, tap STEALTH, CAMO, FLARES, SQUAD, HEAL, WPN / MSL for missiles and bombs, FLIR to scan for silos. Computer: WASD to fly, mouse to aim, click or Space to fire, R or right click launches ordnance, G next weapon, X FLIR, E shield, V stealth, C camouflage, Enter pauses. Start screen: J / K / H switch the jet and duel missile between AUTO and MANUAL and the AI between EASY and NORMAL; 3 continues from your best level. Game controller: sticks to fly and aim, d-pad up for stealth; tap button 7 to launch, hold it with the d-pad to change weapon, use FLIR or pause.
