Domino Game

Trace, topple magic dominoes and sing along: ABC, Hindi and Bangla letters, 65 rhymes, numbers, animals, India, road safety and more, Nursery to Class 7, with English, Hindi or Bangla voice.

A free learning game for children from Nursery to Class 7, with 838 pictures in 15 subjects. Every picture has a real dotted outline to trace: A to Z and a to z (each letter shows its word, A for Apple), the Hindi varnamala and the Bangla barnamala, 65 sing-along rhymes in English, Hindi and Bengali (traditional rhymes and poems by Tagore, Sukumar Ray and others), numbers, shapes and maths, the India map with all 28 states and 8 union territories, monuments, animals, birds, sea life, plants, food, home, feelings, good habits, helpers, vehicles, the world, science and social studies for Class 6 and 7, and road safety with a Cross the Road game. Wonder Town is a palm-shaped island in the sea with a house for every subject. When the outline is traced, dominoes stand up on it; tap and every cascade becomes a new show with its own melody on one of ten instruments, a new rhythm and little pauses, so no two shows sound the same, then the real picture appears with its name. Tap ABC to hear it said and spelled, Sing for rhymes, the note button for a tune, or the picture for the animal's own call. Moving picture guides and a helping hand teach young children how to play without reading. Real-life videos (Wikimedia Commons, checked by hand), Find it!, Spell it, Draw my own, 14 stories, up to six players, a rainbow cap and stickers, and a parents' report. Works offline once loaded. Only an anonymous count of how often the game is opened is kept (GoatCounter, no cookies, nothing about the child). Pictures: OpenMoji (CC BY-SA 4.0) and drawn for the game; maps: Natural Earth. Android app (free): saiqulmodi.github.io/DominoGame/download.html

How to play

Tap once to start. Walk through Wonder Town or tap Pictures, pick a picture, trace the dots with your finger or mouse, then tap to topple the dominoes and watch the magic show. Tap ABC to hear a name spelled, Sing to sing a rhyme with the words lighting up, and the purple note for a tune. The red play button shows a real video. The ? button explains everything with moving pictures; Games has Find it!, Spell it, Cross the Road, Draw my own and stories.
