Garden Bonk

A polished Whack-a-Mole game: 3 rounds of rising speed, a golden bonus critter, and a skunk you'd better not hit.

Garden Bonk is a beginner-friendly Whack-a-Mole game built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no libraries required. Critters pop out of 9 holes across 3 rounds that get progressively faster; whack regular moles for points, snag the rare golden critter for a big bonus, and steer clear of the skunk or lose points. Includes a live score, countdown timer, round indicator, an end-of-game scorecard with accuracy and rank, a saved best score, and a one-click restart. Works with mouse, touch, or keyboard on desktop and mobile browsers.

How to play

Click or tap a critter before it ducks down, or press number keys 1-9 for the matching hole. Avoid the skunk!
