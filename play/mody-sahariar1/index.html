Arrow Rush

Clear every arrow off the board without a collision. 100 classic levels and 10 adventure worlds.

A calm, clever puzzle game. Every arrow points somewhere. Tap to fire it, and it slides until something stops it. Clear the board in the right order and you win the level. Also on the Play Store and App Store.

How to play

Tap an arrow to fire it; it flies until it hits a wall or another arrow. Clear the whole board to win.
