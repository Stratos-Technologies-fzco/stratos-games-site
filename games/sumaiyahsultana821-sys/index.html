How it works: Tap a numbered tile, then tap an adjacent tile with the same number to merge them into one tile worth double. Every merge drops a fresh tile onto the board, so keep chaining matches to climb higher. The game ends when the board is full and no more merges are possible — Restart to try again, or Undo to take back your last move.

How to Play

  1. Select any numbered tile on the board.
  2. Select a neighbour directly beside it (up, down, left or right) that shows the same number.
  3. Matching tiles merge into one tile worth double, and you score the new value.
  4. A new tile appears after every merge — plan ahead to avoid filling the board.
  5. Reach the target tile to win. The game continues afterward if you want a higher score.
  6. Made a mistake? Tap Undo to reverse your last merge.
