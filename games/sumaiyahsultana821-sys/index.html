🏝️ Island Match Quest

Flip, match, and unlock three islands of shells, fruits, and gems.

Best score: 0

How to play

  1. Tap a card to flip it, then tap another to find its match.
  2. A match stays face up and scores points. A miss flips back.
  3. Clear every pair to unlock the next island.
  4. Each island has a move limit. Run out and you get one second chance.
  5. Fewer moves and a faster time earn a bigger bonus.
