🏝️ Island Match Quest

Flip, match, and unlock three islands of shells, fruits, and gems.

How to play

  1. Click (or tab to a card and press Enter) to flip it face up.
  2. Flip a second card to try to find its match.
  3. A match stays face up and scores points. A miss flips both back after a moment.
  4. Clear every pair on an island to unlock the next one.
  5. Fewer moves and a faster time earn a bigger bonus. Use Restart quest to play again anytime.
