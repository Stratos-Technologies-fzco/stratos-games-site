## 2026-11-18 — Phase 3 (animals and production)
Done: master data in JSON (`items.json` 33 items, `animals.json` 6, `buildings.json` 6 habitats + 8 factories, `recipes.json` 17, all frames/icons checked against the atlases). `LocalFarmService` gains placeBuilding (uses the iso `validatePlacement`), upgradeBuilding (3 levels), buyAnimal, feedAnimals, collectAnimals, queueRecipe, collectJobs, all atomic, ledgered, idempotent and queued. Saves migrate v1 -> v2 (nothing lost). World: `BuildingsLayer` draws buildings (windmill sails turn), animals (idle, eat after feeding, wander with walk frames, flipped for down-right), ready bubbles and the placement ghost (green/red diamonds, tap a spot, confirm). UI: Shop (Animal homes / Factories tabs), habitat and factory panels (feed, collect, buy, upgrade, recipes with have/need, queue with countdowns), placement bar. Items panel now lists every product and sells it.
Rules: an animal is hungry -> fed (1 animal feed) -> producing (10-35 min by animal) -> ready -> collected -> hungry again. Happiness: no change for 30 min hungry, then -1 per 10 min, floor 50 (mild offline penalty), +10 when fed; below 100 it slows production by up to 12.5 percent. Production jobs run one after another per building (start = end of the previous job, computed at queue time), inputs are taken when queued, queue slots 2/3/4 by level, capacity 4/6/8.
Decisions: added a starter recipe (3 wheat -> 1 animal feed at level 1) so chickens work before corn unlocks at level 3; buildings appear instantly (the `construction_2x2` sprite is not used yet); animals do not walk outside their pen; "Animals" and "Shop" nav buttons open the shop on their tab.
Tests: vitest 52 passed (iso 12, layout 5, farm service 17, animals/production 18: placement rules, capacity, feeding, offline egg, no double collect under concurrency, partial feed, happiness floor, upgrades, wheat -> flour -> bread, full/locked/missing-input queues, offline jobs, idempotent queueing, ledger, v1 save migration). Playwright 6 passed (new: place coop, mill and bakery through the UI, reject an overlapping spot, buy and feed a chicken, collect an egg, flour twice then bread, reload and check everything is still there). The e2e seeds a save through localStorage for coins/level, which is test setup only.

## 2026-11-18 — Phase 4 (economy and progression, MVP complete)
Done: `src/data/orders.json`, `quests.json`, `farm_layout.json` (expansions); `src/services/quests.ts` (progress derived from cumulative stats counters, so a new quest needs no new tracking code). Service v3 adds fulfilOrder, skipOrder, claimQuest, skipTutorial, expandLand, moveBuilding, removeBuilding; save migrates v2 -> v3.
Orders: up to 4 slots (1, 1, level 3, level 6), reproducible seeded generator, only items the player can really make at their level, first order always 2 wheat from Old Joe, reward = 1.3 x item value, xp = value / 4, a delivered or skipped slot refills after 3 minutes (computed from time, nothing ticks).
Quests: tutorial t1-t5 (plant, harvest, sell, buy animal, finish order), 6 daily candidates (3 picked per day by a stable hash), 9 goals, 6 achievements (levels, harvests, orders, animals). Claims are idempotent and cannot be repeated.
UI: Market (Orders / Sell), Quests panel with badge in the top bar, tutorial banner (hides while any panel is open), expansion panel (tap a locked patch), Move and Remove in every building panel, level-up toast lists what unlocked.
Decisions: expansion materials are factory goods (flour, bread, cheese) because the atlas has no wood/stone icons; no rotate (no art); removing needs an empty building; Items panel and Market > Sell both sell.
Tests: vitest 73 passed (iso 12, layout 5, farm 17, animals/production 18, economy 21: orders, rewards, cooldown, reload, expansion rules, move, remove, quests, claim once, daily reset, tutorial, migration).
