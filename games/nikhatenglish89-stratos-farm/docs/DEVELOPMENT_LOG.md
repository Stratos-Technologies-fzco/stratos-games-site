## 2026-11-18 — Placement fixes (from owner testing)
Problems: (1) double-clicking a home or factory on a selected spot said it could not build; (2) the building ghost moved with the cursor. Causes: a 2x2 building was always placed with the selected tile as its top corner, so any rock, tree or fence one tile down-right blocked it (the island is small and full of decor); and the ghost followed every mouse move while placing; a double-click slower than 300 ms also fell through to the single-click path and closed the shop. Fixes: the selected tile may be ANY corner of the building (the four ways it can cover that tile are tried); the failure message now says why (already something there / locked land / outside the farm); the ghost only moves when you tap, never with the cursor; the double-click window is 400 ms. Tests: vitest 104, Playwright 18 (new: shifted fit, slower double-click, ghost stays put).
