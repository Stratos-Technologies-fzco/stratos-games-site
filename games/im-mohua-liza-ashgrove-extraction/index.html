Phone controls: drag on the left side to move, drag on the right side to look around. Hold FIRE to shoot, tap AIM to aim, tap USE near Mara or items. Play with the phone turned sideways.

Crouch behind low walls, cars and crates to break line of sight. Enemies hear gunfire. If Mara dies, the mission fails. Checkpoints save after each objective.

Characters: Elias is the AvatarSDK MetaPerson sample avatar and Mara is the Avaturn sample avatar, both from the TalkingHead project and licensed for non-commercial use. The militia soldier and motion capture come from the three.js examples (Mixamo).
