GAME MODES

Draw Dominoes

2 players (4 players play without a boneyard)
  • Match a tile to either open end of the chain.
  • No match? Draw from the boneyard until you can play.
  • First to empty their hand wins the round.

Block Dominoes

2 or 4 players (teams of two)
  • Match an open end. No drawing at all.
  • No match? You must pass.
  • If nobody can move, the lowest pip count wins.

HOW TO PLAY

The set

28 tiles, from 0-0 to 6-6. Each player is dealt 7 tiles. With 2 players the 14 left over form the boneyard.

Who starts

Whoever holds the highest double opens with it. No doubles? The highest tile opens.

The chain

The chain is one single line, so doubles do not branch. When it reaches the edge of the table it turns and runs back like a snake, and the tiles on the way back are shown turned round. The two open ends are always the first and last tile.

Your turn

  • Tap a tile in your rack. Tiles you can play glow gold.
  • Tap a gold dashed end on the table to place it. If only one end fits, tap the tile twice.
  • Stuck? In Draw mode tap DRAW until you get a playable tile. In Block mode tap PASS.

Draw vs Block

Draw: you must draw when you cannot play; you pass only when the boneyard is empty. Block: no drawing, you pass.

Winning a round

Empty your hand first and you score all the pips left in your opponents' hands. If the game is blocked (everyone passes in a row) the lowest pip total wins and scores the difference. A tie scores nothing.

Teams (4 players)

You and the player opposite you are a team. Your team's pips are added together and scores are shared.

Winning the game

Reach the target score (100, 150 or 200) first.

Daily challenge

One hard match a day with the same deal for everyone. Play on back-to-back days to build your streak (one missed day is forgiven).

Play a friend online

Tap PLAY A FRIEND ONLINE, then CREATE A ROOM and send the invite link or the 5-letter code. Your friend taps JOIN. The room creator's device runs the game, so keep that page open. In a 4-player room, empty seats are played by the computer and are marked CPU. If someone drops out, a computer takes their seat. Hints and undo are switched off online.

Hints

You get 3 hints per game. A hint shows the move the computer would make.

QUICK TIPS

Three things to know
1. Tap a glowing tile
Gold glow means it fits an open end of the chain.
2. Tap a gold end, or drag the tile there
If only one end fits, tap the tile twice.
3. Stuck?
Tap DRAW until you can play (Draw mode) or PASS (Block mode). First to empty their hand wins the round. Use HINT or UNDO if you need help.
