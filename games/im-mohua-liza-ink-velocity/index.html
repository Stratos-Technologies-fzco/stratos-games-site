How to play

Draw roads with mouse or finger. Ink is limited.
Turbo pen (2) makes boost strips. Costs double ink.
GO (Space) starts the car. You can keep drawing while it drives.
Nitro: hold Shift. Brake: hold S / ↓.
Lean in the air with A / D or ← / →. Land flips for stunts.
R resets the car and keeps your roads. Z undo, C clear.
