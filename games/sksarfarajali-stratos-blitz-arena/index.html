ABOUT THE GAME

The idea

Stratos Blitz Arena is a fast, blocky 6v6 team shooter made for short matches. Two teams fight over one glowing hill. A match takes about three minutes, so you can play one in a break and still feel like you played a whole game.

How a match works

Hardpoint: stand on the hill to score for your team. The hill moves every minute, so keep running. First team to 150 points, or the highest score when the clock ends, wins. Bots fill every empty slot, so a match starts in under 10 seconds.

Your loadout

Pick two weapons and two abilities before you play. Every weapon feels different. Abilities like the dash, shield, grapple and decoy help you escape, flank or win a close fight. Skins are only for looks and never change how you play.

Keep coming back

Earn XP and level up to unlock new weapons. Finish three new daily challenges. Spend Bolts, the coin of this game, on skins in the shop.

Stratos Coins

Stratos Coins are the shared coins of stratos.games. You earn them here when you play and win, and they work across the other Stratos games too. They are separate from Bolts, which stay inside Blitz Arena.
