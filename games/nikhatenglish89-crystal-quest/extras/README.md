# Crystal Quest - The Lost Kingdom: design and asset pack

Everything produced for the STRATOS.GAMES match-3 game, from the master prompt in `Prompt.txt` (left unchanged).

- `docs/crystal-quest/PROMPT_REVIEW.md` - gaps and contradictions found in the prompt, with fixes.
- `docs/crystal-quest/Prompt_Addendum.md` - paste-ready block to add to the prompt before using Claude Code.
- `docs/crystal-quest/UI_DESIGN_SPEC.md`, `design-tokens.css`, `design-tokens.json` - UI design.
- `docs/crystal-quest/AUDIO_DESIGN.md` - music, sound effects and Phaser guidance.
- `docs/crystal-quest/ASSET_INVENTORY.md` - every asset with its honest status (Production / Placeholder / Missing).
- `assets/` - original SVG art (crystals, specials, blockers, characters, bosses, backdrops, icons, logo) and `manifest.json`.
- `audio/` - 8 music themes and 15 sound effects (.ogg and .mp3).
- `previews/` - contact sheets of the art.
- `tools/` - scripts that regenerate the art, audio and inventory.

UI mock-ups (20 boards, in the Design canvas): https://claude.ai/artifact/9M2vqdcDWswQZnNqM1gJid

All art and audio is original and generated for this project. It is placeholder-grade where the inventory says so. Sample numbers on mock-ups are not real game values.
