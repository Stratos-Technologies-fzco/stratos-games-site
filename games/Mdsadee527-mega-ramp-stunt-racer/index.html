HOW TO PLAY

  • Drive: W/↑ accelerate, S/↓ brake-reverse, A/D or ←/→ steer
  • Boost: SPACE — fill the boost meter by landing stunts clean and drifting
  • Air Control: while airborne, A/D rolls and W/S pitches the car — line up your landing!
  • Stunts: flip, roll and spin in the air for points. Land clean (wheels down, low tilt) to BANK the combo. Land badly and you lose it.
  • Reset: R — respawn at the last checkpoint if you get stuck or crash hard
  • Camera: C — cycle camera views
  • Pause: ESC
  • Earn stars in Time Trial mode to unlock faster cars in the Garage.
