    How to play

    • Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn, play one that matches the top card's color, number or symbol.
    • FREEZE next player loses their turn.
    • FLIP reverses direction (with 2 players it works like Freeze).
    • +2 next player draws 2 and is skipped.
    • PRISM plays on anything, you pick the new color. Prism +4 also makes the next player draw 4.
    • Stacking (optional): answer a +2 with a +2, or any draw card with a Prism +4. The total keeps piling up for the next player.
    • Can't play? Tap the deck. If the new card fits, play it or keep it.
    • Down to 1 card? Hit LAST CARD! within 3 seconds or draw 2 as a penalty.
    • First to empty their hand wins.
